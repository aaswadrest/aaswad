Business Improvement & Profit Optimization Plan

Data-backed solutions tailored for Aaswad Pure Veg Restaurant to cut uncontrolled expenses, revitalize sluggish revenue streams, and protect daily working capital.

Health Score 80 / 100 Optimal Operating Health

1. How to Cut & Control Highest Expenses

Target: 10%-15% Cut
Centralized Wholesale Kirana & Cooking Oil: Stop daily retail store purchases. Procure 15kg oil tins and 50kg grain bags in 15-day wholesale cycles from APMC Mandai for an instant 7% to 10% cash savings.
Commercial LPG Gas Conservation: Prepare yellow, red, and makhani gravy bases in bulk morning pressure cookers rather than heating individual small tawa pans per order. De-clog yellow carbon burner nozzles every 15 days to save 18% fuel burn.
Measured Dairy & Paneer Portioning: Pre-cut paneer into exactly 8 standard cubes (100g) per portion dish. Random cutting by kitchen helpers creates a 12% hidden loss.
Staff Advance Cap (25% Limit): Cap advance withdrawals to maximum 25% of monthly salary, released strictly once between the 15th and 20th.

2. How to Boost Lowest Income Streams

Target: +25% Growth
Corporate & Industrial Thali Subscriptions: Pitch executive veg meal boxes (₹90–₹120) to nearby industrial MIDC factories, banks, and IT parks for steady recurring lunch income.
Direct WhatsApp Delivery (0% Commission): Avoid high 25% aggregator commission cuts. Print flyers on parcel packaging: "Order directly on WhatsApp for 10% OFF + Free Gulab Jamun".
Monetize Afternoon Slump (3:30 PM - 6:30 PM): Introduce high-margin teatime snacks: Special Masala Chai + Misal Pav / Crispy Vada Pav / Dosa combos to monetize idle tables and electricity costs.
Weekend Handi Biryani / Family Combos: Promote 1kg / 2kg Family Feast Handi Biryani packs in local housing society groups for instant weekend takeaway sales without occupying dining tables.
